About HolyVibes

HolyVibes Catholic Songs Tracer is a free tool that helps the faithful find Catholic songs they remember — by title, choir, artist, language, lyrics, or keyword.

Our aim is simple: make it easy to discover, listen to, and download Catholic music that has been made available for free distribution by its owners or administrators.

Slogan: Find the Catholic Song You Remember.

HolyVibes does not claim ownership of any song, recording, lyric, or image published on this platform. All content is uploaded by the site administrator only with permission or under a license that allows free distribution. See the Copyright Policy.

Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. What we collect

Visitors may browse and search without creating an account. We do not require personal information to listen to or download songs.

The hosting provider may automatically log technical information such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, as part of normal server operation.

2. Administrator data

The site administrator logs in using Firebase Authentication. Credentials are stored securely by Firebase — never in this public page.

3. Cookies & local storage

This app may store small preferences (e.g., last selected filter) in your browser. No tracking cookies are set by HolyVibes itself.

4. Downloads

Downloading a song does not create an account or collect personal data.

5. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the site administrator using the contact details on the Copyright page.

Copyright & Takedown Policy

HolyVibes Catholic Songs Tracer exists to share Catholic songs only when the administrator has permission to distribute them, or when the material is offered under a license permitting free distribution.

What is allowed

  • Audio recordings uploaded by the administrator with the owner's permission.
  • Lyrics published with the author's or publisher's permission.
  • Cover images that are original, licensed, or public domain.

What is not allowed

  • Commercial recordings uploaded without permission.
  • Lyrics copied from copyrighted hymnals without authorization.
  • Any content that infringes copyright.

Reporting a copyright issue

If you believe content on this site infringes your rights, contact the administrator with:

  1. The song title and link.
  2. Proof of ownership or authorization.
  3. Your contact details.

Valid takedown requests will be honored promptly and the material removed.

Administrator contact: Set your email in CONTACT_EMAIL (top of the script).
